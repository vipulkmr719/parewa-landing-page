1. Who this is from

Parewa is operated by [LEGAL ENTITY NAME], registered at [REGISTERED ADDRESS], India. In the language of India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (the “DPDP Act”), we are the Data Fiduciary and you are the Data Principal — we decide what happens to your personal data, and this policy is our notice to you about that.

This policy covers parewa.com and the Parewa application. It does not cover any other site we link to.

2. What we collect

When you join the waitlist

Three things, all of them typed in by you:

  • Your work email address — so we can tell you when access opens.
  • Your agency name — so we know who you are.
  • Roughly how many proposals you send a month — a range, chosen from a dropdown, so we know who to open access to first.

That is the whole form. There is no hidden field. You can read the page source and confirm it.

When you simply visit

Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs — your IP address, the page requested, the time, your browser's user-agent string. These are generated by the act of serving you a web page and are used for security and diagnosing faults. We do not combine them with anything else or use them to build a profile of you.

Page analytics

We use Vercel Web Analytics to see which pages people read. It is cookieless and it is built not to identify you. With each page view it records: the time, the URL and path, the referrer that sent you, filtered query parameters, approximate location from geolocation, your device type, operating system and browser with their versions, and the version of the analytics script itself.

What it does not do matters more. It sets no cookies. It stores no IP address. It creates no persistent identifier — instead a hash is derived from your request, and that hash is discarded and reset every 24 hours, so you cannot be followed from one day to the next, let alone from one website to another. We see counts and trends, not people.

The script is served from our own domain, so loading this page does not hand your details to an outside address. Vercel processes the results for us as our hosting provider — see section 6.

When you email us

If you write to us, we keep the email. That is how conversations work. Please do not send us anything sensitive that we did not ask for.

3. What we don't do

We measure which pages get read, as section 2 describes. Here is everything we have chosen not to do, stated plainly rather than left for you to assume the worst about.

  • No cookies. This website sets none at all — not for analytics, not for preferences, not for anything. There is no cookie banner on this site because there is nothing to consent to.
  • No persistent identifier. Nothing we use assigns you an ID that survives the day, so there is no thread to pull on that connects one of your visits to another.
  • No stored IP addresses in our analytics. Our host writes standard server logs, described in section 2, and those rotate out.
  • No cross-site tracking, and no advertising networks. Nothing here follows you to another website, and nothing here exists to retarget you with ads.
  • No session recorders or heatmaps. We do not replay your visit or watch your mouse move.
  • No fonts, styles or scripts from anyone else's servers. Every one is served from our own domain. Most sites load fonts from Google, which quietly hands your IP address to Google on every page load. Ours do not — that is precisely why we self-host them.
  • No sale of personal data, ever, and no data brokers. We do not buy contact lists — you will not hear from us because somebody sold us your address.

4. Why we collect it

Under the DPDP Act we may only process your personal data for the purpose you consented to. When you submit the waitlist form you are consenting to exactly this:

  • Emailing you when early access opens, and about your place in the queue.
  • Occasional updates about Parewa's progress before launch.
  • Deciding the order in which we invite agencies in.

Separately, we use the aggregate analytics described in section 2 to understand which parts of this site people actually read, so we can write better ones. That data is counts and trends; it is not tied to you and we could not single you out from it if we wanted to.

Nothing else. We will not use your waitlist details for an unrelated purpose without asking you first. Every email we send you will have a working unsubscribe link, and unsubscribing does not remove you from the waitlist — it just stops the updates.

7. Your proposals and client data

This section applies once Parewa launches. We are stating it now because we say it on our home page, and a promise made in marketing should be findable in the policy.

  • Your content is yours. The proposals you write, the client details you put in them, your templates and your brand kit belong to you. We process them to run the service for you and for no other reason.

  • You can delete your account, and doing so deletes your content from our live systems. Backups are overwritten on their normal cycle, which we will state precisely before launch.

8. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act, as a Data Principal you have the right to:

  • Know what we hold. Ask for a summary of the personal data we process about you and what we do with it.
  • Correct it. Have inaccurate or incomplete data corrected, completed or updated.
  • Erase it. Have it deleted, unless we are required by law to keep it.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, as easily as you gave it. For the waitlist, replying “remove me” to any email from us is enough.
  • Nominate someone to exercise these rights on your behalf if you die or become incapacitated.
  • Complain — to us first, through the grievance officer below, and to the Data Protection Board of India if we have not resolved it.

To exercise any of these, email hello@parewa.com. We will respond within 30 days. We will not charge you, and we will not make you jump through hoops.

One thing the DPDP Act asks us to pass on: you have a duty not to file a false or frivolous complaint, and not to impersonate someone else when making a request. We may need to verify who you are before acting on a request — usually by confirming you control the email address in question.

10. Security

We take reasonable security safeguards to prevent a personal data breach: data encrypted in transit (this site is served over HTTPS) and at rest, access limited to the people who need it, and providers chosen partly on their security record.

No system is perfectly secure and we are not going to pretend otherwise. If a breach occurs that affects your personal data, we will notify you and the Data Protection Board of India as the DPDP Act requires, and we will tell you what happened rather than what sounds best.
